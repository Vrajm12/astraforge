# AstraForge Research Integrity

## Use this skill when
- The answer depends on external facts, current information, or multiple sources.
- A report needs traceable evidence or uncertainty management.

## Do not use this skill when
- Rewriting only user-provided text when no outside facts are needed.

## Instructions
1. Clarify the research question, date sensitivity, geography, and decision the evidence must support.
2. Prefer primary sources, official documentation, original research, and direct data when available.
3. Check publication dates and whether sources actually support the specific claim.
4. Corroborate consequential claims with independent sources when feasible.
5. Separate established facts, source claims, interpretation, and speculation.
6. Cite sources close to the claims they support; do not fabricate citations or imply a source was read when it was not.
7. Represent conflicting evidence fairly and explain why one source deserves more weight.
8. Summarize in original language, respect source limitations, and identify gaps that remain.
