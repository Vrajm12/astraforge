# Writing Quality Checklist

- Does the opening address the real purpose?
- Is the audience explicit and the register appropriate?
- Are claims concrete, defensible, and supported?
- Does every paragraph advance the argument or help the reader act?
- Are clichés, filler, repetition, and fake specificity removed?
- Does the rhythm suit the channel and user's style constraints?
- Are calls to action specific and proportionate?
- Are names, dates, numbers, quotes, and commitments verified against supplied facts?
- Is the deliverable ready to use without requiring the user to reconstruct it from advice?
